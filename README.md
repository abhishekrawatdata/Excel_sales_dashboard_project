# Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the use of Microsoft Excel to build an interactive executive dashboard for analyzing business sales and profitability. It brings key performance indicators, monthly trends, product performance, sales channels, and geographic comparisons into a single view.

**Project type:** Guided Excel dashboard project  
**Tool:** Microsoft Excel  
**Focus:** Sales analysis, profitability analysis, and data visualization

## Business Objectives

The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders:

- Monitor overall revenue, gross profit, gross profit margin, cost of goods sold (COGS), orders, and units sold.
- Identify monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Compare product and category performance.
- Examine revenue distribution across sales channels.
- Explore geographic differences in sales performance.
- Filter results interactively by product category, country, and quarter.

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard displays the following headline metrics:

- Total Revenue: $645,223
- Total Gross Profit: $296,384.25
- Gross Profit Margin: 45.9%
- Total Units Sold: 6,698
- Total Orders: 2,707
- Cost of Goods Sold: $348,839

These figures should be interpreted in the context of the source dataset, reporting period, and active filters.

## Dashboard Features

- KPI cards for headline business metrics
- Monthly revenue and gross profit trend chart
- Top-five country revenue comparison
- Revenue distribution by sales channel
- Region-wise comparison
- Country-level map visualization
- Interactive slicers for product category, country, and quarter
- PivotTables and PivotCharts for summarizing business data

## Preliminary Observations

- June shows the highest displayed monthly revenue, at approximately $70K.
- February shows the lowest displayed monthly revenue, at approximately $36K.
- The reported gross profit margin is 45.9%.
- Coffee accounts for 39% of the displayed category distribution.
- Sales values vary across the products shown in the Top 5 Products chart.

These observations are preliminary and should be validated against the underlying workbook before being treated as final business findings.

## Limitations and Next Steps

This is a guided learning project and not an independently developed production reporting system. Future improvements include validating chart labels and aggregations, documenting the data source and reporting period, analyzing gross profit margins across segments, and adding a concise executive recommendations section.

## Learning Objective

The objective of this project is to develop practical Excel skills and build a foundation for more advanced work in data cleaning, Power Query, SQL, Power BI, and business analytics.
